Anuj Shikarkhane

I am a Research Assistant at the Visual Computing Lab, IISc Bangalore, working with Prof. Anirban Chakraborty on developing DynEval, an evaluation framework for text-to-image generative models that jointly measures prompt alignment and image quality while providing fine-grained feedback on where generated images fail.

I completed my dual degree in Computer Science and Physics at BITS Pilani, where I worked with Prof. Tanmay Verlekar.
